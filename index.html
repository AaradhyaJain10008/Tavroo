Clarity & Details

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about bespoke sizing, our 3D avatar fitting engine, made-to-order craft, and studio shipping.

How does the 3D Runway Atelier work? +
Our interactive 3D Runway Atelier runs natively in any modern web browser using Three.js WebGL technology without downloading apps. You can select your avatar silhouette, rotate 360°, inspect physical fabric drape, test lighting presets, and apply customizable typography overlays in real time.
Why are TAVROO garments priced strictly between ₹799 and ₹2,000? +
We operate on a pure direct-to-consumer atelier model from our Bangalore workshop. By eliminating wholesale distributors, retail rents, and unsold deadstock markups, we deliver luxury 280–350 GSM cottons, tailored vegan leather, and Japanese denim directly to you at honest atelier costs.
How long does made-to-order delivery take across India? +
Once your order is confirmed, our master tailors cut and stitch your piece within 48 to 72 hours. Domestic express shipping delivers across Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, and all Indian metro zones in 3–5 business days with tracked express dispatch.
How do I determine my accurate size before ordering? +
Every product in our Lookbook Catalog includes precise garment dimensions (chest, shoulder, length, waist) in both centimeters and inches. You can also view fit recommendations on the 3D Runway Atelier or connect directly with our Atelier Concierge for bespoke sizing assistance.
Can I request custom embroidery or bespoke corporate collabs? +
Yes! In addition to individual 3D made-to-order garments, we engineer bespoke studio capsule collections for design agencies, art collectives, and private clients. Contact our concierge at concierge@tavro.studio.
